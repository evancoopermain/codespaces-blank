Our story is still rising

North Star Bakery began as a Saturday market stall where baker Mara Ellis sold sourdough and cinnamon rolls. Neighbors asked for fresh bread during the week, so Mara and co-owner Theo Park opened a small shop in Maplewood.

A bakery built around the neighborhood

Today, the team bakes in small batches and keeps seasonal favorites in the case. The name North Star reflects the owners' hope that the shop can be a familiar neighborhood landmark.

How we choose ingredients

We choose flour from regional mills when available, use seasonal fruit, and bake in small batches. Ask our team about ingredients or allergy concerns before ordering.

The people behind the counter

Mara Ellis, Head Baker

Mara plans the daily bake and looks after the breads. Her favorite is the country sourdough, especially when it is warm from the oven.

Theo Park, Counter Lead

Theo welcomes customers and coordinates pickup orders. He helps families and event planners choose treats for their gatherings.
